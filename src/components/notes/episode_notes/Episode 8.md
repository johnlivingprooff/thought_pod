#Opener 

Why do we still feel this existential dread, even when we know what to do? 

Why does the knowledge become a burden rather than a solution to our problem? 

I think it lies with how we believe as humans.. we have this tendency to mirror whatever is placed in front of us the longest.. what I mean is that for most people, we are so unconsciously reflective of our surrounding environment, that we begin to act like what we're conversant with, and I say conversant, because conversant insinuates that we are not just seeing and hearing, we are actively participating in the spaces that grow us into what we be-live (become, and live out).

So, why do we dread existence? - I came across someone who said; "people become what they do subconsciously, when they are not paying attention to what they consume", so our dread of existence might be tied to our subconscious lack of intentionality with how we live our lives 

When you don't actively participate in the formation of your thoughts, then decisions, you are inevitably led astray by the dictates of what surrounds you - so knowledge becomes a burden, not because it isn't powerful enough to help you, but, because it doesn't make you understand, you decide to understand the information you consume, you decide to find solutions to your problems from what you've seen and known. The decision is yours to make with what knowledge you have.

So, the light switch.. why do you turn it on.. you'd think the answer is obvious, but why? Oh.. so that there's light in the room, why do you need light in the room? so that I can see the people and things in the room, feel connected and make conversations.. why do you need to feel connected and make conversation? so I can belong, be a part of the whole.. why do you need to belong? because it's scary to be alone in a cold and depraved world..

You see how seeking understanding, brings deeper meaning to a mundane task. Just choosing to understand what you know by poking at it with intentional questions, brings you the clarity you never would glance at on a regular day. I believe life becomes interesting when you choose to seek an understanding of what you know.

## Don't let anything kill your curiosity. It's one of the vital lifelines of humanity.
