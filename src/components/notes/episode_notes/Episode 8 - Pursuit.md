Purpose is the intention of your creator, as He designed all he made. His intention was for it to be good, because when he saw all he made, he called it good. His Purpose/intent was to create good. 

To understand purpose is to understand God's intent for your existence, so that when He says all things will work out for the good of them that love him, it is because they choose to walk in His intended purpose for them. 

So, the pursuit of Purpose is to understand and follow the intentions and plans that God has prepared for His own, from the beginning of time. And this demands that we know the plans He has. Jer29:11; his plan is to prosper and not harm you, to give you a hope and future. 

His intent is to love what is Good, so he makes what is Good, so that He can love on what is Good. 

When you encounter the purpose of God for you, it is confirmation of what you know, then an elevation/expansion to what you need to know. 

Romans 8:28-39.
