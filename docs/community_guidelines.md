# Thought Notes — Community Guidelines

## Purpose of This Space

*Thought Notes* exists to explore the human experience through reflection, conversation, and creative expression.

This is not a platform for performance, outrage, or dominance.
It is a place for **thinking in public**, together.

We welcome:

* uncertainty
* disagreement
* unfinished ideas
* stories still being understood

What matters here is **how** we speak, not whether we agree.

---

## Who This Space Is For

This space is for:

* listeners of *The Thought Podcast*
* hosts and guests
* readers who resonate with reflective inquiry
* anyone willing to engage with curiosity, honesty, and respect

You do not need credentials to contribute.
You do need **care**.

---

## What You’re Encouraged to Share

You are welcome to post:

* Reflections inspired by an episode
* Personal stories or lived experiences
* Questions that don’t yet have answers
* Disagreements expressed thoughtfully
* Creative responses (short essays, poetry, observations)
* Faith, doubt, philosophy, or meaning-making—spoken from your own perspective

Speak **from your experience**, not on behalf of everyone.

---

## How to Participate Well

### 1. Speak *With*, Not *At*

Write as if the person reading is human—because they are.

Avoid:

* mockery
* condescension
* rhetorical cruelty

Clarity is encouraged. Hostility is not.

---

### 2. Disagreement Is Welcome — Disrespect Is Not

You may challenge ideas.
You may question assumptions.
You may strongly disagree.

You may not:

* attack individuals
* reduce others to labels
* dismiss lived experiences as invalid

Critique ideas. Honor people.

---

### 3. Stay Grounded in Good Faith

Post with the intention to:

* understand
* contribute
* reflect

Not to:

* provoke
* dominate
* derail
* advertise

This is a thinking space, not a battlefield.

---

### 4. Be Honest About What You Know

Present opinions as opinions.
Beliefs as beliefs.
Experiences as experiences.

Do not present speculation or misinformation as fact.

---

### 5. Respect the Shape of the Space

* Notes are for **standalone reflections**
* Comments are for **responding to a specific note**
* Episode-linked notes should meaningfully relate to the episode

This helps keep the space readable and alive.

---

## Anonymity & Identity

You may post:

* anonymously
* under a name
* under a pseudonym

Anonymity is a **permission to be honest**, not a license to harm.

Repeated misuse may result in moderation or posting restrictions.

---

## Moderation & Stewardship

This space is moderated to protect its purpose.

Moderators may:

* review submissions
* remove content that violates guidelines
* archive or limit repeated disruptive behavior

Moderation is not censorship of ideas.
It is care for the community.

---

## Content That Is Not Allowed

The following will be removed:

* harassment or personal attacks
* hate speech or dehumanization
* spam or promotional content
* deliberate misinformation presented as fact
* sharing private or identifying information about others

This list is enforced consistently, regardless of viewpoint.

---

## A Shared Responsibility

This community works only if we:

* listen carefully
* write thoughtfully
* assume good intent, until proven otherwise

If you’re unsure whether something belongs here, pause—and ask:

> *Is this contributing to understanding, or just adding noise?*

---

## Closing Note

*Thought Notes* is a living archive of thought in motion.

No post needs to be perfect.
But every post should be **considerate**.

Thank you for thinking here.
